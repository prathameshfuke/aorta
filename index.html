Wrappers break when the model changes. Hospitals can't.

  • A new model ships every few weeks

    A prompt wrapper is tuned to one model's habits. Each release moves the output, and the product moves with it.

  • Billing math is not a guess

    Room-rent caps, proportionate deductions, non-payable lists and statutory clocks need the same answer every time, with a rule you can cite.

  • The plumbing is the hard part

    HMS, LIMS and EHR systems, TPA portals, WhatsApp voice notes, and rules like DPDP, ABDM, NHCX and NABH. Nobody large will maintain every local connector.
