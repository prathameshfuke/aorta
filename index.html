    How a claim moves through Aorta

    1. Reads the claim

      Scans, PDFs and TPA letters become structured lines. Patient names are masked first.

    2. Checks the claim line by line

      Each line meets the rule that covers it. The model reads. The rules decide.

    3. Holds the claim for approval

      The dispute cites every rule. A person approves it before it is sent.

    Questions about Aorta

    Who is Aorta for?

    Hospital billing teams, TPA and NABH consultants, diagnostic labs and insurance advisors. We are starting with claims.

    Does an AI model decide the claim amount?

    No. A model reads the documents. Written rules calculate every amount and name the rule they used.

    What happens to claims when a better model arrives?

    The model is one line of configuration. The rules, approvals and audit trail stay as they are.

    Where does patient claim data go?

    Names are masked before any model sees them. Free models only ever see synthetic or anonymized data.

    What if a model finds a new claim deduction pattern?

    A person checks it, then we write it as a rule. From then on it runs as code.
