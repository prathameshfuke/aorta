## Does anything leave my runner?

Nothing goes to Aorta. Live parity sends fixture prompts to your model providers. When Claude runs the skill, the files it reads are sent to the Claude model. See [Security](/security).

## Does an AI decide whether a migration is safe?

No. Schema, label, numeric and cost checks are deterministic, and they decide the result.

## What if there is no codemod for a finding?

Breakline reports it as needing a human. Shopify token changes are handled this way.

## Which languages are scanned?

Python and JavaScript/TypeScript, plus config and env files.
